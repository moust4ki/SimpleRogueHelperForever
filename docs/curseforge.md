# SimpleRogueHelper - Forever

**Energy marks for rogues on WoW Forever.** On the game's own personal resource display, up to five
thin marks on your energy bar, each at the energy you set: dim below it, **lit in gold once you have
that much energy**. Put one at your Eviscerate's cost and no more guessing whether it will go off.

> **To get started**: turn on the *Personal Resource Display* in the game's Edit Mode, then type
> `/srh` and set your marks.

## Features
- **Your values**: five marks, each from 1 to your maximum energy (less one) or none, set in the
  window. Fixed: the addon reads no spell cost, nothing moves them but you. Kept per character.
- **On the bar itself**: the marks follow the game's energy bar, shown, hidden and scaled with it, and
  are placed again when your maximum energy changes or when you resize the bar in Edit Mode.
- **In combat too**: where the game hides power values from addons, the marks stay in place and are
  lit by the game itself.
- **Everything on the enemy (option)**: your combo points and your energy, with its marks, right on
  your target's health bar, above or below it, at the spacing and size you choose, with your energy
  as a number if you like, drawn like the personal resource display: no more looking back and forth
  between the enemy and the personal resource display. Tick it first in the window (`/srh`).
- **Gamepad or mouse**: a small settings window driven with the D-pad, made for WoW Forever's
  gamepad play.
- **Five languages**: English, French, German, Spanish and Italian.

## The settings window

Open it with `/srh`, from *Escape › Options › AddOns › SimpleRogueHelper - Forever*, or with a key of
your own (*Key Bindings › AddOns*).

| Gamepad | Mouse | |
|---|---|---|
| D-pad up / down | hover | move |
| A | click | check / uncheck an option; on a value, the next one |
| D-pad left / right, on a value | wheel over it, right click | change it (held: on and on) |
| B | the × button | close |
| LB / RB | wheel | previous / next page |

The window closes when a fight starts, and doesn't open in combat.

## Installation

Install with the CurseForge app, or extract the zip into
`World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/`.
**Fully restart the game** after installing (a `/reload` does not load a new addon).

## Compatibility

Made for **WoW Forever**. Works on its own, with or without
[Easy Controller - Forever](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/easy-controller-forever).
